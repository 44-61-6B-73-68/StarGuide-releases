Privacy policy

Last updated 21 November 2026

In short

StarGuide runs on your computer. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising, and it does not collect or send usage data. Your photos and settings stay on your device.

Optional features that use the internet

Two features connect to the internet, and only when you switch them on:

  1. Online solving. If StarGuide cannot recognise a photo offline and you have enabled this, a downscaled copy of that photo is uploaded to nova.astrometry.net, marked private, using your own API key. Their privacy policy applies to that service. It is off by default.
  2. Satellite orbits. Public orbit data is downloaded from celestrak.org. Nothing about you or your photos is uploaded.

What is stored on your computer

Settings, presets, a cache and (if you allow it) a list of recently opened photos are kept in your user folder. You can clear the recent list in Settings, and uninstalling the app removes its data.

This website

This site does not use cookies or tracking scripts. Our host, Cloudflare Pages, may keep ordinary server logs.

Children

StarGuide does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
